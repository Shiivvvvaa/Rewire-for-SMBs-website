One real example

One client. Two builds. Look at the hours.

A gym and health group with many branches.

Before

Notes done by hand. A report no one trusted.

Trainers sent their notes by chat. The front desk typed them in again. A long list of old leads just sat there.

The build

A live board, and a bot that phones old leads.

What the trainer types is the record. There is no second copy to fix. The bot works the old list on its own and books the ones who want to come in.
